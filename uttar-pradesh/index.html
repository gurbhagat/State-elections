Frequently Asked Questions

When is the UP Assembly Election 2027?
The UP Assembly Election 2027 is expected to be held in February–March 2027. The Election Commission of India will announce the official schedule approximately 2–3 months before polling. Dates on this site are estimates only.
How many seats are there in the Uttar Pradesh Vidhan Sabha?
The Uttar Pradesh Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) has 403 seats spread across 75 districts. Of these, 84 seats are reserved for Scheduled Castes (SC) and 2 seats are reserved for Scheduled Tribes (ST). A party or alliance needs at least 202 seats to secure a simple majority and form the government.
Who won the 2022 Uttar Pradesh election?
The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) won a landslide victory in the 2022 UP Assembly Election, securing 255 of 403 seats with a 41.29% vote share. Yogi Adityanath was sworn in as Chief Minister for a second term. The Samajwadi Party (SP) emerged as the primary opposition, winning 111 seats with 32.06% vote share.
What was the 2017 Uttar Pradesh election result?
In the 2017 UP Assembly Election, the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) won a historic landslide, securing 312 of 403 seats with a 39.67% vote share. Yogi Adityanath became Chief Minister. The Samajwadi Party (SP) won 47 seats, and the Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP) won 19 seats.
How many districts does Uttar Pradesh have for assembly elections?
Uttar Pradesh has 75 administrative districts for electoral purposes. The largest district by number of assembly seats is Prayagraj (formerly Allahabad, 12 seats), followed by Azamgarh and Kanpur Nagar (10 seats each).
What is the voter turnout history in Uttar Pradesh elections?
Uttar Pradesh typically sees active voter engagement. The 2022 election recorded a voter turnout of 60.6%, whereas the 2017 election recorded 61.24%, and the 2012 election recorded 59.4%.
Who are the main political parties in Uttar Pradesh?
The major political parties active in Uttar Pradesh are: the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP, current ruling party), Samajwadi Party (SP), Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP), Indian National Congress (INC), and Rashtriya Lok Dal (RLD).
How can I find results for my constituency?
Use the Constituencies page to search by name or district. Each constituency page shows the 2022 and 2017 winner, vote margins, turnout, and a full candidate breakdown. You can also use the Results pages for a complete list sorted by any column.
